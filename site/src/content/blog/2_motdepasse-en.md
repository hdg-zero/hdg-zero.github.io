## Why Password Managers Matter

In an era of ubiquitous online services and recurring credential leaks, relying on reused or simple passwords presents significant security risks. A dedicated password manager combined with strong, unique credentials and multi-factor authentication (MFA) remains one of the highest-impact security habits anyone can adopt.
