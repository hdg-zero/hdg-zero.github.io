## Welcome

This is my first publication here, serving to explain the mission and ethos behind this digital garden.


## Why a Dedicated Space?

Working in systems engineering, cybersecurity, and infrastructure over the past several years, I have participated in a wide array of projects, explored diverse architectures, and continuously learned from both successes and failures.

Yet as months and years roll by, hard-won lessons and nuanced architectural decisions fade from memory if they are not consistently exercised or documented. For this first reason, I wanted a permanent, independent platform to write freely and document the areas I care deeply about: Linux systems, privacy, container security, and local AI workflows.

Furthermore, engineering experiments frequently remain scattered across private repositories or informal scratchpads. This portfolio provides a structured, publicly verifiable record of my work, giving fellow engineers and collaborators an honest look into how I think, build, and solve technical problems.


## What You'll Find Here

The platform is split into two complementary tracks:
- **The Blog**: Deep-dive essays, technical retrospectives, and conceptual analyses on software architecture, privacy, and system internals.
- **Projects & Case Studies**: Full-scale project breakdowns detailing the architecture, security models, source code, and trade-offs of my open-source work.
